Review date: 2026-11-27

## Newly reviewed issuer-primary document

- Issuer: Sampada Laghubitta Bittiya Sanstha Limited
- Document: 8th Annual Report, FY 2080/81 (2023–2024), audited financial statements and notes
- Issuer-hosted PDF: https://www.sampadalaghubitta.com.np/upload/document/1745558125-book%20compile_compressed.pdf
- Extracted evidence:
  - Notes to the financial statements, reporting entity section: “The joint operation commenced on July 14, 2024.”
  - Events after reporting date: the company states that it started joint operation as Sampada Laghubitta from Ashadh 30, 2081.
- The report's financial statements were authorized for issue by the Board on 2081.11.11; the report is for FY 2080/81.

## Interpretation

This is direct issuer-origin evidence, in an annual report containing audited financial statements, that the merger's joint operation began on **2024-07-14**. It independently corroborates the existing Priority97 date and contemporaneous 2024 market announcements.

A separate NEPSE-hosted issuer notice reviewed earlier prints **2082-03-30 (2025-07-14)**. The two documents conflict. The annual report materially strengthens the 2024 date for the corporate joint-operation event, but it does not by itself prove the exact NEPSE suspension date, final trading date of SABSL/SDLBSL, first exchange-trading date of SMPDA, or historical stable security IDs.
